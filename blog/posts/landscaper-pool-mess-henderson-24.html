When Landscaping Makes a Mess of Your Pool

The short answer

Ask landscapers to blow away from the pool and empty baskets after their visit. That is the core of good pool debris cleaning for a desert pool—not a one-time dump of store chemicals.

What is going on with the water

Blowers push dirt and leaves straight into the water. In Southern Nevada, sun, heat, hard water, and dust stack on top of normal swimming. A pool that stays clear in a mild climate can struggle here without steady care.

Mature landscaping in Cadence makes this a weekly issue. That is why generic internet tips often fall short for Henderson homeowners.

What you can do this week

  1. Look at the water in daylight. Can you see the main drain clearly?
  2. Empty the skimmer and pump baskets. Full baskets make the pump work harder and the water turn dull.
  3. Check the filter pressure if you know your “clean” number. A big jump usually means the filter is loading up with dirt.
  4. Do not guess with random products. If the pool is already green or the equipment sounds wrong, call a pro.
  5. Keep a weekly cleaning plan. Skipping weeks in peak heat is how small problems become multi-day cleanups.

How professional pool service helps

A solid weekly pool cleaning visit is more than a quick skim. Your tech should brush, empty baskets, test the water properly, balance chemicals for the week ahead, and glance at the equipment pad.

If the water has already turned, you may need green pool cleanup. If the pump, filter, or salt system is the weak link, pool equipment repair comes first—chemicals cannot fix a system that will not circulate.
