What to do this week about saltwater pool service

  1. Step 1. Check the salt cell inspection window or error lights monthly.
  2. Step 2. Test salt and stabilizer—not just free chlorine.
  3. Step 3. Clean the cell when output drops instead of only raising salt.
  4. Step 4. Watch for white scale on the cell plates in hard water.
  5. Step 5. Keep weekly service so small cell issues do not turn into green water.
