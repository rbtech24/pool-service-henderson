Can I fix a green pool myself in Henderson?
Light green sometimes, with good equipment. Fully green water usually needs multiple visits and filter work.
How long does green pool cleaning take?
Often several days of brushing, filtering, and chemistry—not one afternoon.
Is green water unsafe?
You cannot see the bottom well, which is a safety issue for kids and pets. Get it clear before swimming.
