What to do this week about pool chlorine problems

  1. Step 1. Stop adding more tablets if eyes burn and the smell is harsh.
  2. Step 2. Test free vs total chlorine if you can—or call for a pro test.
  3. Step 3. Run the filter after a proper shock plan.
  4. Step 4. Empty baskets; organic load makes combined chlorine worse.
  5. Step 5. Shower before swimming when possible—sunscreen and sweat add to the load.
