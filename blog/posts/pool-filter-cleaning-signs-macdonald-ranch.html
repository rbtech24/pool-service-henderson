What to do this week about pool filter cleaning

  1. Step 1. Write down clean filter pressure after a service visit.
  2. Step 2. Watch for a steady climb of 8–10 psi or more.
  3. Step 3. Empty baskets first—pressure can rise from a full basket too.
  4. Step 4. Do not ignore return jets that feel weak.
  5. Step 5. Book a deep clean if pressure returns quickly after a rinse.
