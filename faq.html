Henderson Pool Service FAQ

Straight answers on chemistry, scheduling, HOAs, and recovery.

What parts of Henderson do you serve?
Green Valley, Anthem, Inspirada, MacDonald Ranch, Seven Hills, Cadence, West Henderson, Lake Las Vegas area, and nearby corridors. If you’re on the fence geographically, ask — route density decides.
How is Henderson different from Las Vegas pool care?
Many Henderson neighborhoods sit higher or catch more wind off the mountains. Debris patterns and sun exposure differ from the central valley. We route and dose for local conditions, not a generic metro script.
Do you offer free estimates?
Yes for standard weekly service profiles. Complex commercial or long-neglected green pools may need an on-site look before firm pricing.
Can you take over from another company mid-season?
Absolutely. We’ll baseline chemistry, note equipment condition, and transition without a multi-week “learning curve” tax if access is ready.
What’s your green pool process?
Test, kill algae correctly, support filtration, clean as water clarifies, and rebalance. We don’t dump random shock and vanish for ten days.
Do you service community / HOA pools?
Select small commercial and HOA accounts when scheduling fits. Ask about CPO needs and testing frequency requirements.
Are pets a problem during service?
Secure pets please. We love animals; we don’t love surprises near the equipment pad. Gate notes help.
How fast can you start service?
Often within 1–2 weeks depending on route capacity in your neighborhood. Green-to-clean may start sooner.
Do you sell retail chemicals only?
We’re a service company first. Chemicals are included as needed for maintained accounts; we’re not a warehouse storefront.
What payment methods do you accept?
Card and ACH autopay preferred for routes. Invoices are itemized — service vs. repairs clearly separated.
Will you winterize?
Southern Nevada is a year-round swim market for many homes. We adjust runtime and chemistry seasonally rather than “close” most pools, unless you request a true shutdown.
How do I report a problem between visits?
Call or text the office line. Route techs check messages; emergencies (flooding, major leaks) get same-day triage when possible.

Ready for clearer water in Henderson?

Tell us about your pool — size, spa, salt or chlorine, and access. We’ll reply with a straightforward plan.
